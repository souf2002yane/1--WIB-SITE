Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Applicable law: Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data (Dahir 1-09-15 of 18 February 2009) and the supervision of the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel).

1. Who is responsible for this website?

Soufyane El Haddaoui (SEH World), Morocco. Contact for any privacy question: souf2002yane@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • SEH World has no server and no database. The profile you create is saved only in your own browser (localStorage) on your own device.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data.
  • We use no advertising and no tracking cookies. Optional anonymous statistics (Plausible) are used only if you click “Accept” on the banner.

3. What information is used and why

DataWhere it is keptPurpose
Profile form on the INFO page: first name, family name, phone number, age, email, password, address/city, gender, marital status, hobby, languages, free text, date/time, profile photoYour browser only (localStorage)To show your profile, let you log in on this device and personalise the site
Points, level, game results, leaderboard entryYour browser onlyTo run the games and the leaderboard on your device
Theme choice (light/dark) and cookie choiceYour browser onlyTo remember your settings
Anonymous visit statistics (page, country, browser type — no cookie, no personal profile, IP address not stored)Plausible Analytics (only if you accept)To understand how many people use the site and improve it

Because the profile data never leaves your device, we cannot see it, read it or recover it for you.

4. Legal basis

Your consent (Law 09-08, article 4): you choose to fill in the profile form and you choose to accept or refuse optional statistics. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account on the INFO page, clearing your browser data, or using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

5. How long data is kept

Profile and game data stay on your device until you delete them (button “Delete My Account” on the INFO page, or clearing your browser data). Plausible does not keep individual visitor profiles.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

  • Strictly necessary browser storage (localStorage): profile, points, theme and your cookie choice. No consent is needed for these because you ask for the feature, and they stay on your device.
  • Plausible Analytics: cookie-free, loaded only after you accept.
  • We use no advertising cookies and no social-media tracking pixels.

7. Other services you may contact

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host it receives technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads a page.
  • Third-party images: some game pictures on the GAME page are loaded from other websites, which can see your IP address when the picture loads.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) delivers a script used by the “download story as image” button.
  • WhatsApp / Facebook are opened only if you click a share button.
  • When a security check (Cloudflare Turnstile) is enabled on a form, Cloudflare processes technical data to tell humans from bots.

Some of these providers are located outside Morocco (for example in the European Union or the United States). We only use them for the purposes above.

8. Security — please read

Your data is stored in your browser, so anyone who can use your device and browser may be able to see it. Do not use a password here that you use anywhere else (email, bank, social networks), and do not enter sensitive information. Use a private device or log out/delete your account on shared computers.

9. Children

SEH World is a general-audience entertainment website. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before filling in the profile form.

10. Your rights (Law 09-08, articles 7 to 9)

You have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the use of your personal data. Most of this you can do yourself on the INFO page (“Edit My Info”, “Delete My Account”). For anything else, write to souf2002yane@gmail.com. You also have the right to file a complaint with the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, we will update this page and its date. See also our Terms of Use (CGU).
